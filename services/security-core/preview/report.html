About KORVNT

KORVNT is a daughter company of Nortivo. Service enquiries help determine the scope of an assessment or design project. Evidence preservation, tracing and authorized coordination depend on the case. AI-assisted website design is scoped as a project. Continuous monitoring has no verified operating coverage.

Framework and control status

Nortivo KORVNT is the technical framework used by Nortivo Security. Its controls are integrated into Nortivo applications and tested locally. KORVNT is pronounced kor-vent.

Website and APIs

Tested locally

The application enforces same-origin checks, CSRF tokens, input limits and shared rate limits.

Accounts and administration

Tested locally

The server checks current account status and ownership. Named administrators require verified accounts and fresh multi-factor authentication.

Trading platform

Tested locally

Private workspace access is checked server-side. Provider rights, broker consent and live-order release have separate checks.

Private data

Partial coverage

New private messages and briefs are encrypted for their record and owner. Earlier tickets, portfolios and backups have different protection paths.

Privacy and responsibilities

Nortivo maintains the application controls and tests. Hosting, database, email, payment and market-data providers operate their own systems. Production retention, key recovery, backups and alert delivery still require verification.
