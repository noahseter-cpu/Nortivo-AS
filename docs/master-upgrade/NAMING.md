# KORVNT naming screen

Registry checks on 18 November 2026. The revised brief names the customer-facing daughter company **KORVNT**, its security-service identity **Nortivo Security** where appropriate, and the underlying framework **Nortivo KORVNT**.

The owner reports purchasing korvnt.no through Uniweb. The domain search indicated an occupied domain and did not identify its owner. Norway’s joint service returned an uncertain trademark result, and similar names are already in use. A release decision still needs a jurisdiction-specific review of the expanded services. Domain ownership establishes neither legal registration nor trademark clearance. This review made no registration or configuration change.

## Before using the name publicly

Record the intended markets, languages, emblem and all five service areas, including web design and case-specific cybercrime assistance. Search exact, prefix, phonetic and visual variants including KORVNT, KORVENT, KORVANT and KORVENTIS. Review Norwegian national marks and international designations covering Norway. Review EU, international and US records as applicable to the actual markets, including pending filings and registered marks. Examine the actual goods/services, dates, status and territory for each potentially similar word or figure. Downloadable software and hosted software/security services typically direct attention to Nice classes 9 and 42. The product description and any genuinely offered financial services determine whether additional classes require review. [Norwegian registers](https://www.patentstyret.no/sok-databaser) · [TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/) · [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/) · [WIPO Nice Classification](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/classification-nice) · [USPTO trademark search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search)

The TMview and WIPO public entry points were accessible. A complete, reproducible cross-register similarity search or legal assessment was outside this preliminary record. Save the dated result exports, classes, territories, near-match dispositions and reviewer decision before release. A public security wordmark should describe the verified scope of the product.
