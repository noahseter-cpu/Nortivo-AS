# Restaurant demonstration

User explicitly requested a professional fictional restaurant on Nortivo as a public demonstration, and asked for direct implementation without previews in this ongoing build.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Lune is a fictional neighbourhood bistro. Visitors can understand the menu and try a reservation interaction; no real restaurant, customer relationship, opening hours or booking availability is claimed.

OWN-WORLD: cool blue invitation-paper fields, ivory-white reading surfaces, deep blue-green ink. Booking actions use dark ink; pale yellow is reserved for the closing Nortivo enquiry action. Locally served Manrope, large compact headings and open menu rows rather than cards. The generated food photograph provides the warmth.

STORY: enter the bistro, browse categories and vegetarian choices, select a date/party/time and see an explicitly synthetic confirmation. Return to Nortivo to discuss a real project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: narrow always-visible demo notice; Lune wordmark and short anchor navigation; a blue text half with a large two-line invitation and booking/menu actions; a full-height food photograph on the other half. Mobile retains the demo notice, readable actions and one photograph below the invitation.

FORM: supper-club invitation, candidate 7, seed 584bca8c. Other considered structures: menu folio, kitchen pass, neighbourhood noticeboard, cookbook spread, table-setting still life and market board. Poster, terminal, lighting cue, origami, zine and drum-machine challengers were declined: their navigation obscures the ordinary menu/booking task. Kept disciplines: poster hierarchy, terminal explicit state, lighting separation, origami recoverability, zine explanatory clarity, rhythm-machine visible selection. No motifs copied. Code-led under the user's direct-implementation preference.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

- All restaurant names, dishes, prices and opening-hour examples are invented and visibly labelled demo content.
- No reviews, ratings, real address, phone number or staff biography.
- No personal data fields, network requests, database storage or payments in the reservation demo.
- Menu filters and confirmation remain keyboard accessible. No motion on frequently changed menu data.
- Norwegian and English content parity, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support.
