## Consolidation only after a written quote

Ask Alpaca whether one commercial package can cover US prices and news for **all paying Nortivo users, including users without an Alpaca account**, plus non-display processing, external AI and caching. Its $99 individual data plan is not evidence of redistribution rights; Broker Standard offers IEX real time or delayed SIP, with comprehensive coverage requiring separate arrangements. Its calendar is market sessions, not macro releases. Keep the current adapters until the scope and total price are confirmed. [Market-data plans](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api), [news entitlement](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/news-3), [market calendar](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/calendar-2)

Finnhub can also quote news plus macro calendar, but Enterprise calendar inclusion is not publicly established. Its documented calendar lacks stable event IDs, revision timestamps, source links and explicit timezone metadata, so it is not a drop-in replacement for Trading Economics.
